## Remaining Risks

| Severity | Item | Boundary / next step |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | Curated economic and green-space indicators span different years and administrative boundaries. | They are visibly marked `REFERENCE · MIXED YEARS`. Move them into a typed per-value provenance registry before causal or policy claims. |
| Medium | Current AQI and weather depend on Open-Meteo availability. | Failed responses render as unavailable and never fall back to fabricated values. Add a second cited provider only where licensing and units match. |
| Low | Main, Firebase, MapLibre, and worker bundles exceed Vite's 500 kB advisory threshold. | Split non-comparison dashboard features further; Split Compare is already lazy-loaded. |
| Low | Live traffic is available only when a Mapbox token is configured. | The traffic control stays absent without a token; do not imply traffic coverage when it is unavailable. |

## Product Boundary

The system can compare any built-in or user-resolved city spatially. That does not mean every city has the same quantitative coverage. Current environmental observations, reference indicators, and absent values remain distinct in the interface so visual comparison does not become a false claim of causal correlation.

## City Explorer Upgrade

- Each map now opens a docked city explorer with Today, What if?, and Evidence views. Desktop retains the map alongside the explorer; phones use the map workspace for the explorer with a visible close button.
- Today connects city context to current air quality, apparent temperature, and wind. Evidence links to the public providers and labels CAMS air quality and weather as model estimates.
- The green-space scenario conserves existing area, converts added hectares to square metres, and adjusts for a user-selected population change. It reports per-resident area and the share of the city boundary needed. It makes no cooling, access, health, or economic forecasts.
- Missing green-space baselines remain unknown. Invalid population and area inputs are rejected. Exports include the scenario inputs, outputs, available condition snapshots, retrieval timestamps, provider links, and boundary limitations.
- Comparison feeds now refresh every ten minutes and use the underlying provider cache timestamps. The previous wrapper cache could relabel old provider data as newly retrieved; it has been removed.
- The existing systems/planning surface is reachable from the explorer with its assumptions visible.
- Primary provider documentation checked: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs and https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api. CAMS is a regional atmospheric model, not a street sensor network.
